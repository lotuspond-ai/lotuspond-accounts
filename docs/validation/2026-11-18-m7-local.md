# M7 validation: the switch-over, rehearsed, 18 November 2026

**Not run on production.** Every gate in `docs/cutover.md` is open: the privacy review, production Accounts (no AWS access), and Health #40 and #48 unmerged. This is a rehearsal with synthetic people, on the local stack, with LotusPond Health from `lotuspond-health` branch `claude/sleepy-hamilton-zqrfgd` (PR #48, `20b55de`).

## The rehearsal (`tools/cutover-rehearsal.sh`), run twice, passing both times

1. **Health as it is today** (no `ACCOUNTS_ISSUER`). Three people, A, B and C, registered with Health passwords.
2. **The move, with the real commands of both products.**
   - Health's `accounts:export`: "3 accounts written".
   - Accounts' `accounts-cli import`: "Created 3, already imported 0, already linked 0, skipped 0".
   - Health's `accounts:link`: "linked 3, already linked 0, conflicts 0, not on this server 0".
3. **Health with `ACCOUNTS_ISSUER`, passwords still on:**
   - A signed in at Accounts with their old Health password, was asked to confirm the email, confirmed it, and landed in their **own** Health account (same id as in step 1).
   - B still signed in with their Health password.
   - D, someone new, signed up through Accounts and got a new Health account.
   - A then reset their password at Accounts.
4. **Rollback** (`ACCOUNTS_ISSUER` unset):
   - C and B signed in with their Health passwords.
   - **A's old Health password worked again, and A's new Accounts password did not.**
   - **D could not sign in to Health at all.**

   These are recorded as the rollback's known effects in `docs/cutover.md`, with the decision they call for.
5. **The watch report** (`accounts-cli report`) showed the day's sign-ins, new accounts, confirmed emails and the reset, and "Imported from Health: 3; confirmed their email: 1; signed in to Health since: 1", with no email or account id.

## Also checked

- `npm run check`: 66 tests, with a new report test. It checks per-day counts, the failure rate, the imported progress, and that no email or account id appears.
- CI's `health` job now runs the rehearsal on every push.
