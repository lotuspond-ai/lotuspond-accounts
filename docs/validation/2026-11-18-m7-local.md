## The password decision, built and rehearsed

The owner chose option 3 in `docs/cutover.md`. It is built in `lotuspond-health` #48.

**Found by the rehearsal.** The first version refused the Health password of every account *linked* to Accounts. But `accounts:link` links every imported account during the move. So switching on would have refused B's password, for B who had not moved yet, and on an app without the Accounts sign-in, B would have been locked out. The rehearsal failed on exactly that (`409 !== 200`). Health now records the first Accounts sign-in (`users.accounts_signed_in_at`, migration 0020) and refuses the password only after it.

Rerun on the fixed branch, all passing:
- A, moved, is refused their Health password while switched on (409), and it works again after a rollback;
- B, imported but not moved, keeps theirs;
- D, Accounts only, cannot sign in to Health after a rollback;
- Health's 384 server tests and 19 app-client tests;
- the 8 cross-product tests.

**Also found:** Health PR #40 was merged into Health's `main` on 18 November 2026. Another session then merged `main` into #48, and renumbered its ID-token migration to 0019 after `main`'s 0018. The merge here was clean, and the migrations run in order: 0018, 0019, 0020.
